Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data CV Fit AI (“we”, the website at cvfitai.com and its Android app) collects, why, who processes it on our behalf, and how you can access or delete it. Questions: support.

What we collect and why

DataWhyHow long
Email address and password (stored only as a BCrypt hash) To create and secure your account and send service emails Until you delete your account
The resume you upload and the job description you paste To generate your tailored resume and optional cover letter. The uploaded file is read in memory and not stored; its text is sent to OpenAI for processing Not stored after processing
Tailored resumes, cover letters and the first 200 characters of each job description To show your history so you can download them again Until you delete them with your account
Generated PDF and DOCX files To let you download the result Deleted automatically after 24 hours
Credit balance and payment records (plan, amount, date, status, Stripe reference) To add purchased credits and for accounting As required by tax and accounting law
IP address and request details To protect the service against abuse (rate limiting, kept only in memory) and in server logs for security and troubleshooting Memory: up to 24 hours. Logs: a limited period

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you sign up for (performance of a contract), to keep payment records (legal obligation), and to protect the service against abuse (legitimate interest).

Who processes data for us

  • OpenAI generates the tailored resume and cover letter from your resume text and the job description. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Stripe processes card payments on cvfitai.com. We never see or store your card details.
  • Google delivers our emails (Gmail) and serves the fonts used by the pages (Google Fonts), which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server and database where your account data is stored.

Some of these providers are located in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Cookies and local storage

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. The site also stores a small offline page on your device so the app can show a message when you have no connection, and the Android app remembers for the current session that it is running as an app. None of this is used for tracking.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with BCrypt, password reset links are single-use and stored only as hashes, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. You can delete your account at any time from your Account page (see how to delete your account), or ask us by email. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

CV Fit AI is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced by email.

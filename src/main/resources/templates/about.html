About CV FIT AI

Automatically tailor your resume to any job description using AI-powered analysis and intelligent keyword matching.

✦ What It Does

CV FIT AI takes your resume (PDF or DOCX) and a job description, then uses advanced NLP and AI models to create a tailored version optimized for that specific role. The system:

  • Reorganizes your experience to highlight the most relevant skills and achievements
  • Extracts and emphasizes keywords that match the job posting
  • Rewrites sections for better impact and ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimization
  • Provides a summary of all changes made for your review

📋 What You Need to Provide (Inputs)

  • Resume File: PDF or DOCX format • Maximum 10 MB • Must contain your work experience, education, and skills
  • Job Description: The text from the job posting • Maximum 3,000 words • Paste the full posting for best results
  • Output Format: Choose PDF or DOCX • Defaults to PDF but you can select either
  • User Account: Optional for anonymous use • Required to save history and manage credits

📦 What You'll Receive (Expected Output)

  • Tailored Resume: Download in both PDF and DOCX formats instantly
  • Changes Summary: A detailed breakdown of modifications, including rewritten sections and keyword additions
  • Keyword Extraction: A list of important terms from the job description that now appear in your resume
  • Version History: Saved in your account (if logged in) for future reference and comparison
  • Google Docs Integration: View and edit your tailored resume directly in Google Docs

⚡ Credits & Pricing

CV FIT AI operates on a credit-based system:

  • Cost per tailor: 1 credit per resume processing
  • New accounts: Start with 1 free credit to test the service
  • Purchase credits: Buy credit packages from the Credits page at any time
  • No expiration: Credits never expire and can be used whenever you need
💡 Pro tip: Save credits by refining your job descriptions. More specific descriptions lead to better results.

⚠️ Best Practices & Limitations

  • Security: Don't include sensitive information (ID numbers, passwords, bank details) in your job description
  • Always review: Check the AI output manually before submitting applications—human verification is crucial
  • Accuracy: While AI is powerful, it may occasionally modify dates or job titles—verify all details
  • Context matters: More detailed job descriptions produce better-tailored resumes
  • Keep originals: Always keep your original resume for reference and future use

Delete your CV Fit AI account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from the website or from the Android app.

How to delete it

  1. Sign in to CV Fit AI.
  2. Open Account (or go to cvfitai.com/account).
  3. Under Delete account, enter your password, type DELETE and confirm.

You're signed in: go straight to account deletion.

Can't sign in?

Reset your password from Forgot password, or email support from the address you registered with, asking for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and password hash
  • Your resume history: tailored resumes, cover letters and job description excerpts
  • Generated PDF and DOCX files
  • Password reset links and any remaining credits

What is kept

  • Payment records (date, amount, plan and Stripe reference), kept for accounting and tax purposes and no longer linked to your account
  • Server logs, which may contain your email address, are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting, then removed

See the privacy policy for details.

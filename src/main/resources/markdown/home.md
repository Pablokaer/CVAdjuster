# CV Fit AI: your resume, built for the job

CV Fit AI rewrites your resume for a specific job posting with AI. Upload your resume, paste the job description, and get a tailored, ATS-friendly resume in seconds. It keeps your facts and your voice.

Website: https://cvfitai.com/

## How it works

1. **Upload your resume.** Add your current resume as a PDF or DOCX file, up to 10 MB. Its text is extracted so the AI works with your real experience.
2. **Paste the job post.** Add the job description, up to 3,000 words. The AI picks up the role's keywords and priorities.
3. **Download the result.** Get a rewritten resume as PDF and DOCX, with a summary of the changes and the keywords used. You can also ask for a cover letter based on the tailored resume.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which file formats can I upload?

PDF and DOCX files up to 10 MB. Scanned PDFs may fail, because the text has to be selectable.

### Will the AI invent experience I don't have?

The AI is instructed not to invent experiences, certifications or skills. It reworks the wording and emphasis of what is already in your resume. Always review the result before sending it.

### What happens to my resume?

Your resume text and the job description are sent to OpenAI to generate the tailored version. Generated files are temporary and removed automatically, and the result is saved in your account history.

### How do credits work?

Each generation uses {{resumeCost}}, plus {{coverLetterCost}} more if you also ask for a cover letter. New accounts get 1 free credit, and if processing fails the credits are refunded automatically.

### Does ATS-friendly mean I will get the job?

No tool can promise that. CV Fit AI aligns your resume with the job's keywords and avoids formatting that ATS software handles poorly, which helps your resume get read.

# About CV Fit AI

CV Fit AI tailors your resume to any job description, using AI analysis and keyword matching.

## What it does

CV Fit AI takes your resume (PDF or DOCX) and a job description, and uses AI models to create a version optimized for that role. It:

- Reorganizes your experience to highlight the most relevant skills and achievements
- Extracts and emphasizes keywords that match the job posting
- Rewrites sections for better impact and ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimization
- Gives a summary of all changes for your review

## What you provide

- **Resume file:** PDF or DOCX, up to 10 MB, with your work experience, education and skills
- **Job description:** the text of the job posting, up to 3,000 words. Paste the full posting for best results.
- **Output format:** PDF or DOCX. PDF is the default.
- **Account:** a free account, needed to generate resumes, keep your history and manage credits

## What you receive

- **Tailored resume:** download it as PDF and DOCX
- **Changes summary:** the rewritten sections and the keywords added
- **Keywords:** the important terms from the job description that now appear in your resume
- **History:** every result is saved in your account
- **Google Docs:** open the tailored resume in Google Docs
- **Cover letter (optional):** a letter for the job, written from your tailored resume, as PDF and DOCX

## Best practices and limitations

- **Security:** don't include sensitive information (ID numbers, passwords, bank details) in the job description.
- **Always review:** check the result before you send applications.
- **Accuracy:** the AI may occasionally change dates or job titles, so verify every detail.
- **Context matters:** more detailed job descriptions produce better-tailored resumes.
- **Keep originals:** keep your original resume for reference.
